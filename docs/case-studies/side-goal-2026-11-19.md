## Reading

- On a breakpoint provider (`mock-bp`), explicit checkpoints are most of the saving: serial turns pay full input on every request; marking the system prompt and the tail cuts cost by 67–74%. Stems match checkpoints on a short core tail and pull ahead as the core goes on (−76% vs −74% at 8 turns): the core no longer carries the side goal's bulk.
- On an automatic-caching provider (`mock-1`), checkpoints change nothing (there are no marks to set), and stems cost 1–3% more: their extra requests (open, wrap-up) cost more than the cheap cache reads they avoid.
- On both, stems cut the core's context to about a third (14–17k chars to 3–6k). Where cost is flat, that is the gain: room in the context window and a terser core to reason over.
- Caveats: mock replies are canned and tokens are estimated at 4 chars per token; real models vary reply length, may not pull handles or fold well, and real caches have minimum sizes and expiry.
